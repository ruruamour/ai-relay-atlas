<a id="relay-data"></a>
## Relay fields

### relays.csv

`domain` is the canonical hostname and `api_host` is the pricing host when different. `name`, `system`, and `version` describe the public service. `discovered_via` records the discovery route; `veridrop_score` is optional. `http_status` and `latency_ms` describe a homepage observation when available. `last_checked` is the recorded check date, while `tags` and `notes` can include manual annotations.

`n_models` counts the quote rows retained by the atlas. A value of `0` means there are no stored quotes for the site, even if a public service page lists models. `families` and `aliases` are derived from the quote and alias tables. `backend_group` is a clue from identical cross-brand quote tables, not proof of common ownership. See the [detailed Chinese field reference](README.md#relayscsv) and the [metadata-only submission map](../CONTRIBUTING.en.md#metadata-only).

### aliases.csv and relay-models.csv

The alias header is `alias,canonical,reason,discovered_via`. `reason` is `same-host`, `mirror`, or `merged`. For false merges use [split.txt](relays/split.txt), one hostname per line with an optional `#` comment, and rebuild.

The model table joins on `domain` and stores both the original `model` and computed `model_base`. `billing` is `token` or `call`. `input_usd_m` and `output_usd_m` are USD per million tokens; `call_usd` is USD per call. `group`, `group_ratio`, and `model_ratio` record pricing context; `source`, `flag`, and `last_checked` record provenance, price signals, and the quote snapshot date. Common source labels include `newapi:/api/pricing` and `sub2api:/api/v1/model-plaza`. See the [detailed quote fields and flags](README.md#relay-modelscsv).

### Manual annotations

`annotations.csv` uses `domain,tag,note,source,date`. Record a checkable fact with its source and date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). `dedupe.py` carries tags and notes into the relay table. An unusually low quote can justify a review, but does not establish model substitution.

<a id="shop-data"></a>
## Shop fields

The catalogs use `platform,type,domain,note` for platforms and `system,type,repo,note` for systems and relay software. The generated CSV headers are below.

| File | Header |
|---|---|
| [shops.csv](shops/shops.csv) | `shop,hosting,platform,system,system_type,domain,categories,price_range,source,last_checked,notes` |
| [products.csv](shops/products.csv) | `shop,host,hosting,platform,system,category,item,price_cny,status,warranty,stock,source,source_url,last_checked` |
| [candidates.csv](shops/candidates.csv) | `domain,status,likelihood,title,systems,common_hits,ai_hits,relay_overlap,last_checked,browser_review,rendered_title,notes` |

`hosting` is `SaaS` or `自建` (self-hosted). A shop may list multiple hosts. `price_range` uses the CNY prices available in the product data. `source_url` comes from the raw record, and `last_checked` currently reflects the normalizer's batch date. `relay_overlap` matches a relay hostname or alias; a high fingerprint score or a browser review is still a lead to verify product type and availability.

<a id="shop-inputs"></a>
### Shop normalization inputs

`normalize_shops.py` reads `google-card-offers.csv`, `google-ultra-offers.csv`, `openprice-offers.json`, `aihaotan-goods.json`, and `shuita-shops.json` from `data/raw/`. It classifies hosts using its own mappings and `shop-fingerprints.json`. The reference catalogs and candidate review CSV do not feed that normalization. To appear in shop or product data, a reviewed listing still needs a supported source. See [shop-source coverage](shops/sources.md).

<a id="evidence"></a>
## Source evidence

The [source registry](raw/sources/README.md) and [dated batches](raw/sources/batches/README.md) link the hostname snapshots and per-site reviews. Review records preserve the domain, check date, public directory and provider pages, a factual summary, and a homepage status code if observed. `merge.py` takes a `scan.py`-format JSON file passed on the command line. Directory reviews are for human review. In the candidate index, `validated` means the domain has a probe record or is in the relay table. `in_relays` marks canonical inclusion and `page_category` records page classification.

## Monitoring data

The target list has one hostname per line. History records the observation time as `ts` and public status endpoint results as `http_status` and `latency_ms`. `has_model` indicates whether a matching model appeared in that round's public pricing data. `groups`, `min_usd_m`, and `min_call_usd` record the advertised groups and lowest quotes. Report corrections with the timestamp and evidence without rewriting the old observation. `monitor/keys.txt` is local and Git-ignored.

## Conventions

- Prices are estimates from public sources, not audited. The estimated CNY cost is the nominal price × `topup_cny_per_usd`, the disclosed CNY cost of 1 USD in relay credit. Missing prices are blank, not zero.
- Default rankings exclude `promo`, `zero`, `default`, `low`, and missing prices. A quote flagged only `high` remains eligible. A model page requires records from at least 20 sites and a rankable quote.
- `query.py` reads local CSVs. See [offline price queries](../tools/README.en.md#offline-price-queries) for the options.
